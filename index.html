Case Study

Toplitz Productions: Community Velocity Strategy

The Problem: A mid-sized European publisher wanted to convert background interest into measurable community momentum on X (formerly Twitter) leading up to a major seasonal content update for their flagship simulation franchise.

The Strategy: I took over daily optimisation and community engagement on X, deploying content emphasising user-generated narratives ("Live your story. Build your legacy."). I focused on high-conversion visual elements, structured community reply strategies, and organic pacing to turn historical simulation players into active advocates.

Results (30-Day Sprint):

  • 57.2K total impressions generated entirely via organic content positioning (↑ 18.7%)
  • 3.6% average engagement rate by transforming generic broadcasts into interactive dialogue (↑ 32.1%)
  • 1,042 trackable link clicks driven directly to the game's storefront and community hubs (↑ 19.3%)
Case Study

Manifold Garden: Release Asset & Creator Strategy

Client Challenge: A solo developer needed targeted launch support for a mind-bending puzzle game seven years in the making — with a non-Euclidean concept requiring careful positioning for non-gamer press.

Our Approach: Optimised Steam page messaging for broader, non-gamer appeal; designed a high-conversion social media asset kit featuring infinite-loop gameplay GIFs; and built a curated creator outreach list targeting aesthetic and architecture-adjacent audiences.

Results: The asset kit and creator list secured key coverage during launch week, positioning the game as a work of high art across a successful multi-platform release.

Case Study

VistaHaven: Property Showcase & Lead Engine

Client Challenge: A real estate agency needed an elite web portal that would present high-value properties with credibility and capture premium agent leads without feeling generic or template-built.

Our Approach: I engineered a modern, curved container layout featuring dynamic stat counters, custom agent profile carousels, and high-visibility interactive call-to-action touchpoints — built for both visual impact and lead conversion.

Results: The portal elevated the agency's perceived prestige, streamlined property browsing, and created clearly defined lead capture touchpoints that increased qualified agent enquiries from day one.
