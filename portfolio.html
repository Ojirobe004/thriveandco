Case Study · Social Media

Toplitz Productions: Community Velocity Strategy

The Problem: A mid-sized European publisher wanted to convert background interest into measurable community momentum on X (formerly Twitter) leading up to a major seasonal content update for their flagship simulation franchise.

The Strategy: I took over daily optimisation and community engagement on X, deploying content emphasising user-generated narratives ("Live your story. Build your legacy."). I focused on high-conversion visual elements, structured community reply strategies, and organic pacing to turn historical simulation players into active advocates.

Results (30-Day Sprint):

  • 57.2K total impressions — organic content positioning only (↑ 18.7%)
  • 3.6% average engagement rate — generic broadcasts transformed into interactive dialogue (↑ 32.1%)
  • 1,042 trackable link clicks — driven directly to storefront and community hubs (↑ 19.3%)

Case Study · Social Media

Three Wild Women Boutique: Instagram Growth Strategy

The Brief: An Ottawa-based fashion retailer serving women aged 35+ needed to transition from static product photos to high-performance discovery formats on Instagram.

The Strategy: I restructured their content engine around short-form Reels, shifting focus to real-life styling transformations and age-positive fitting room demonstrations. Early organic winners were backed with modest, targeted ad spend to amplify reach beyond their existing audience.

Results (31 Days):

  • +287 new followers — a +25.2% follower growth rate
  • 78.1% non-follower brand reach — content consistently reaching discovery audiences
